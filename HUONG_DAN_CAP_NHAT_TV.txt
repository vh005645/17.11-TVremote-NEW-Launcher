HUONG DAN - CUNG 1 APK CAI CA DT + TV, KET NOI QUA LAUNCHER
================================================================
Doc file nay truoc khi test 4 tinh nang moi.

1) KIEN TRUC THAT (de khong lam sai)
------------------------------------
- APK RemoteTV (vn.herotran.tvcontrol, file zip nay) cai ca tren DIEN THOAI va TV.
- Tren TV, launcher com.ts.launcher dang GIU cong 8999 (TCP + UDP).
  No nhan lenh CU: set_limit, stop_limit, power_off/on, go_home/back,
  volume_up/down, sync_cloud_config, sync_gemini, sync_telegram, sync_weather.
- Vi vay code moi KHONG tranh cong 8999 voi launcher.

2) CACH 4 TINH NANG MOI CHAY (khong can sua launcher)
------------------------------------------------------
- Them TvReceiverService trong CHINH APK nay. Khi APK chay tren TV, service nay:
  + Nghe cong MOI 9000 (TCP) - khong dung 8999.
  + Poll Firebase node /tv_control/command.json moi 2 giay.
  + Chi xu ly LENH MOI:
      take_screenshot, unlock_limit, lock_screen, unlock_screen,
      show_message, hide_message.
  + Lenh CU de launcher xu ly (tranh tat TV 2 lan, tang am 2 lan...).
  + Neu TV chi cai RemoteTV, khong co launcher chay -> Receiver tu xu ly
    CA lenh cu (standalone). Tu nhan biet bang cach thu ket noi 127.0.0.1:8999.

- Dien thoai gui lenh MOI di CA 2 CONG 8999 + 9000 (LanHelper.sendNewCommandBothPorts).
  Ben nao hieu thi chay. Lenh CU giu nguyen chi gui 8999.

- unlock_limit (quan trong):
  Receiver xoa timer cua minh + forward stop_limit ve 127.0.0.1:8999
  de launcher cung huy dem gio cua no + ghi file unlock_limit.json
  vao /sdcard/com.ts.launcher/. Neu khong forward, launcher van tat TV
  theo gioi han cu du DT da bam "Mo khoa".

- Chup anh:
  LAN: DT thu 9000 truoc, that bai thi thu 8999.
  Cloud: Receiver chup bang "screencap -p", nen JPEG 70, ~1280px,
  day Base64 len /tv_control/screenshot.json, DT poll ve nhu cu.
  Neu TV khong chay duoc screencap (thieu quyen shell), DT se bao loi ro.

- Khoa man hinh / Tin nhan:
  Receiver mo TvOverlayActivity (Activity fullscreen, KHONG can quyen overlay):
  + lock_screen: nen DEN TUYET DOI, chu "Tat TV ngay !" trang 56sp, chan BACK.
  + show_message: nen DEN 50% #80000000, chu TRANG 32sp, cham de tat.
  + unlock_screen / hide_message / go_home: dong Activity.

3) CAI DAT + BAT
----------------
- Cai CUNG 1 file APK nay len ca DT va TV (ghi de ban cu).
- Tren TV: mo app RemoteTV 1 lan, bam "Bat nhan lenh" trong card
  "CHE DO TV - NHAN LENH" (tren TV that, app tu bat khi mo).
  Sau do TV tu chay lai Receiver moi khi khoi dong lai TV (TvBootReceiver).
- Tren DT: khong can bat card TV MODE. DT la ben DIEU KHIEN.
- Dong bo lai cau hinh Cloud 1 lan tu DT (nut Luu cau hinh) de TV co
  Firebase URL/Secret moi (Receiver + launcher cung doc file
  /sdcard/com.ts.launcher/firebase_config.json).

4) TEST NHANH
--------------
- LAN (cung Wi-Fi): DT chon "Mang LAN", Quet TV, bam tung nut moi.
- Cloud: DT chon "Qua Internet", bat 4G, bam tung nut moi.
- Khoa: TV den + "Tat TV ngay !" -> bam "Mo khoa man hinh" phai het den.
- Tin nhan: go "Con oi tat TV di ngu" -> TV hien nen den mo chu trang
  -> bam "Xoa tin nhan" phai mat.
- Mo khoa gioi han: dat gioi han 1 phut -> bam "Mo khoa gioi han"
  -> TV khong tu tat sau 1 phut (nho la Receiver da forward stop_limit cho launcher).
- Chup anh: LAN tra ngay, Cloud cho 2-10s.

5) NEU KHONG CHAY
-----------------
- TV chua cai APK moi -> cai lai APK moi len TV.
- TV chua bam "Bat nhan lenh" -> mo app tren TV, kiem tra card TV MODE
  bao "Dang nhan lenh".
- TV chua cap quyen / khong chay screencap -> chup anh bao loi, 3 tinh nang
  con lai van chay (khoa, tin nhan, mo khoa khong can screencap).
- Launcher van tat TV sau khi mo khoa -> kiem tra Receiver co chay khong,
  vi chi Receiver moi forward stop_limit cho launcher.
